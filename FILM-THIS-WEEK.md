# Film this week (Nov 23–29, The Down Week)

A proper off week with no riding: Houston Mon–Tue, fly home to Maine Tuesday. One Down Week episode posts every day at 10:00, made from the **day before's** footage. Film each day's bit by evening and drop it in `inbox/` (from Maine, AirDrop to the laptop or upload wherever you can). About 5–10 minutes of filming a day. Themes are proposals: swap any of them for what the day actually is.

Why it looks like this: the reels that grew the account last week were **voiceovers of a real story with another person in it** (the three sprint-zone reels: 955–1,442 reach, 17–18 s average watch, +9, +7 and +4 followers on the days they posted). With no group rides this week, the episodes lean on you talking plus whoever's around (Trey, family). Your talking and series reels get the best engagement but less reach. **Open on the funniest or most real moment**, with the board hanging over it, not on an intro (setup openings held 54–60% vs ~76%).
