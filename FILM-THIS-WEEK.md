# Film this week (Nov 10–15, for the Nov 16–22 reels)

Six things, about 15 minutes in total across the week. The ★ ones are what next week's reels can't exist without. Phone vertical, 4K/60, back to the wind. Drop clips in `inbox/` before Friday morning's ingest; anything from the weekend by Sunday.

Why these: the library has 31 unused lines of you talking, but only 3 usable reaction shots, no usable proof shot (a readable computer screen) and 4 openers left, and the two new series have no episode filmed yet.

## ★ 1. Road to 5 W/kg · ep 2 — looking back at Monday and Thursday, filmed Sunday

Ep 1 ends on "this week should flow nicer", so ep 2 is whether it did. Monday's "warm-up done, we're doing 2x20 today" selfie is the setup, and Thursday's clip of your teammate is the effort. Sunday is a look back, said in the past tense. Don't present the 3-hour ride as the workout.

Answer these to camera on Sunday, one answer each, no script:

- **How did Monday's 2x20 go?** The honest version, numbers if you remember them.
- **What was Thursday, and where did it turn?** If "cannot count to six" is a story, tell it.
- **So did the week flow nicer, and what's next?**
- *(Optional)* your face for 3 s at the end of the 3 hours, no talking.

## ★ 2. What my roommate wakes up to · ep 2 — getting Trey on a bike

The story across the series is whether Trey ever rides. Every episode opens the same way and ends on his answer:

- **The hallway:** phone low or in your hand, 4–5 s of Pokey's freehub buzzing as you roll to the door. Quiet hallway, no talking over it. This is the series' theme tune.
- **The wake-up:** your line on the way in (last time: "ride done, time to wake up my roommate").
- **The ask:** "you riding today?" or however you'd really say it.
- **Trey's answer:** 5–10 s, on camera or just heard. Don't set it up or retake it; whatever he says is the episode.

Check Trey's fine with being in these before you film.

## 3. One thing that isn't the bike

Pick one: what you actually eat after the ride (10 s, with a line about it), or one sentence the next time Houston calls something "cold".

## 4. The intro, for pinning (once, 20–30 s)

To camera, standing still, quiet room: who you are, that you started a year ago, that you're racing collegiate in the spring, and the 5 W/kg goal. Say it two or three ways. This becomes the first pinned reel on your profile.

## 5. Meet the bike: Pokey's glamour shots (about 30 s, once)

For the "you're never going to win a race, your bike is too slow!" → "the bike in question" reel (caption: "pokey my beautiful"). The existing bike clips are too shaky or too far away to carry the reveal.

- A slow, steady circle around Pokey at bike height, in good light (golden hour if you can).
- Two or three close-ups: the teal paint, the down tube logo, the wheels.
- Spin the back wheel so the freehub clicks, with no talking over it.
- *(Optional)* 2 s of you looking offended at the camera.

## 6. The commute, for "squeezing through Houston traffic"

Run the 360 cam under the saddle (or the GoPro facing back) on your real rides to and from campus, every day this week. Just let it record. Don't chase close passes or stage anything: whatever traffic really does is the reel. At the end of a ride, if something happened, say one honest line to camera about it.

## Answer these to camera when they're true

No script. Just answer the question out loud:

- What's today's workout, and which part are you dreading?
- What did your roommate say?
- What would you a year ago think of this ride?
