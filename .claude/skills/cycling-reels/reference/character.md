# Who's in the reels: Leif

[captions.md](captions.md) says how text is written. This file says who is talking. Read it before choosing lines, writing hooks and captions, or planning a series, so the account sounds like one particular person and not "a cyclist".

Everything here comes from Leif: what they said in this project's conversations, their answers on 2026-11-10 (two rounds), and their own words in the transcripts (`library/transcripts/`). Nothing here is a fact to put on screen by itself: numbers and places still follow the "don't invent facts" rule. When something isn't listed, ask rather than guess, then add it.

## The short version

A Rice sophomore from Maine, living in Houston, who started cycling just over a year ago and is spending the offseason getting ready for spring collegiate racing. Leif trains before class, narrates the workout to the phone, and is honest when one goes badly: stoked to be out there, and making fun of that in the same breath.

## What Leif is working toward

- **Spring collegiate racing: road races, crits and time trials, starting mid-February 2027.** It's the offseason now (November 2026): there are no races to show, so the story is the build toward February. Don't write as if a race is coming this week, and don't make race-recap formats until the season starts.
- **5 W/kg.** Leif gave the numbers on 2026-11-07: FTP 335 W at 78 kg, 4.29 W/kg, and 5 W/kg is 390 W. Watts are the number, never weight loss. Flat and failed weeks are part of it ("progress is not linear but it is upwards").
- Leif is new: "started cycling just over a year ago." That's an asset: learning in public, not teaching from above.

## How Leif sounds

Three registers, all picked by Leif on 2026-11-10:

| Register | From the footage | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hyped and earnest | "let's get it, baby" · "we made it happen" · "one left, let's make it happen" · "today's gonna be a good day" · "see y'all out there" | Openers, the start of a workout, the end of a good one |
| Dry and self-deprecating | "at least it's pretty. well, I'm suffering" · "try my best not to throw up, I'm gonna lay down for a bit" · "episode one is me failing a workout" | The turn of a story, punchlines, captions |
| Chaotic with the crew | Group-ride banter, where the speaker isn't always Leif: "every bridge up is a sprint zone" · "say hello, Shrek. they can see you" · "I took like a nice 300 watt pull and then I was like, oh my god, why did I do that" | Group-ride reels, overheard reels, the roommate |

Habits worth keeping because they recur in the footage:

- **Opens like a broadcast.** The time and the plan: "morning, it is 7 a.m.", "bright and early, 6:52 a.m.", "today we're doing 3x12 sub T", "we are back on the track". Then a weather report.
- **"Pretty", "beautiful" and "a little gray" are about the weather and the light** (the sky, the morning), unless the camera is plainly on the bike ("at least look how pretty the bike is").
- **Says "we"** for the rider and whoever is watching: "we've got two hours zone two today".
- **Counts down out loud** and reports the number: "interval one done, 345 watts, two minutes rest".
- **Every ride ends back at school**: "time for some cool down and then back to school".
- Swears when it hurts. Bleep it, don't cut it.

## What Leif doesn't sound like

- **Not bragging.** On 2026-11-09 Leif pulled "first in my bloodline to wake up early on purpose" as "a little much" and wrote "i should be asleep right now" in its place. Undercut the effort; never present getting up early or training hard as a flex.
- **Not a complainer.** Leif did not pick "annoyed at small things" as their humor. The elevator and the cold can show up, but as a passing beat, not as the personality.
- **Not a coach.** Tips come from "what I got wrong last week", never "you should".
- No borrowed grandiose trend lines, no hype words (captions.md).

## Using this when planning

- **Pick lines that sound like the table above** before lines that are merely clear. A line in Leif's register beats a tidier one.
- **Write hooks from what Leif said**, shortened, not from a stock format. "at least it's pretty" is a better hook than "pov: intervals in houston".
- **One register per reel**, and across a week use all three.
- **Each week, at least one reel shows something from "Outside the bike".**
- **When Leif rewrites text, that's a voice sample.** Record it with `./reels review feedback --category preference --kind text-edit` (both versions, in their words), and add the pattern here once it has happened twice. Changes to this file need Leif's OK.
